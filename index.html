The deal

Usually free. Always up front.

The whole point of this is keeping good material in use and out of a landfill, so free is where I start. Here's the honest math on when it isn't.

Free — the default

Good things cost you nothing

Usable, fixable or recyclable — I want it and you don't pay a dime. That's the deal I'd rather make every time.

When there's a charge

A little over what the dump costs

Some loads carry real junk, and the landfill charges me by weight plus fuel to get there. If that's your load, I'll quote you before I come out — a bit over what the dump run and gas actually cost me. Nothing hidden, nothing added later.

Good offsets bad

Value in the load changes the math

If there's something worth having mixed in, I'll often eat the disposal cost and haul the whole thing anyway. Tell me everything that's going — the good stuff is what makes the rest work.

Questions

What people ask before they text

Is junk removal really free?

Most of the time, yes. If your load is usable, fixable or recyclable, I haul it for nothing — that's the default and it's what I'd rather do. A charge only comes up when a load carries material that has to go to the landfill, because the dump bills me by weight. In that case you get a number from me before I drive out.

Do you take scrap metal and appliances?

Always, and there's no condition requirement. Steel, aluminum, copper, brass, roofing tin, fencing, pipe, car parts, water heaters, batteries, washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves — rusted and broken is fine. Metal is the one category I'll never turn down and never charge for.

What areas do you cover?

Crestview and about 60 miles out in every direction — Okaloosa, Walton and Santa Rosa counties. That covers Fort Walton Beach, Niceville, Valparaiso, Destin, Navarre, Milton, Baker, Laurel Hill, Holt, DeFuniak Springs, Freeport and everything in between. If you're near the edge of that, ask anyway.

How fast can you pick up?

Most pickups happen within 24 to 48 hours of your text. If you're on a closing date, a move-out or a landlord deadline, say so and I'll work around it.

What won't you take?

Household garbage and bagged trash, rotted or mold-damaged furniture, demolition debris like torn-out drywall and shingles, and hazardous material — paint, chemicals, fuel, tires. I'm a recovery outfit, not a dumpster service. Send a photo if you're unsure and you'll get a straight answer.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Not usually. If the items are outside, in a garage or somewhere I can reach, tell me where and I'll handle it. Big or awkward loads are easier if somebody's there to point at things, but it isn't required.

Can you clear out a whole house or estate?

Yes. Whole-room, garage, shop, rental turnover and estate clear-outs are a big part of what I do. Text photos of the rooms and a rough idea of the timeline and I'll tell you what it takes.

Will you trade instead of taking cash?

Happily. I'll trade for items and I'll trade for work — hauling against something you need done, or something you can do that I need. Just bring it up when you text.
